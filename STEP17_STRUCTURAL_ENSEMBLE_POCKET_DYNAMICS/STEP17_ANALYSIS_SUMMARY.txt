and D166V PNPLA3 using smooth collective coordinate perturbations.

IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
------------------------
The ensemble analysis provides a computational assessment of
relative structural mobility and persistence around the D166/D166V
region.

It should not be described as molecular dynamics, binding-energy
analysis, free-energy analysis, or experimental validation.

The results can be used to prioritize regions for subsequent
therapeutic compound screening, docking, and later MD analysis.
